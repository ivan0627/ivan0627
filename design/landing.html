Straight answers

The fine print.

Can't I just delete the app?

In strict mode, Lockout locks your settings for the period you chose — on iPhone that includes shielding Settings and the App Store, the same mechanism Brick uses. No consumer app can be literally impossible to remove (anyone claiming otherwise is lying), but getting out early takes a 24-hour cooling-off period or an emergency key your crew can see. The friction is the feature.

What if I can't make the gym today?

The daily challenge has your back: 7K steps before sunset, 300 active calories, any 30-minute workout — verified through your health data, not the honor system. Rest days exist too: schedule up to two a week, 24 hours ahead so it's a plan, not a relapse.

What do you do with my location?

It's processed on your phone. Our servers store "trained 47 minutes," never a map of your life. Your gym's address is never visible to anyone — your crew sees that you trained, never where.

And my health data?

Read-only, used for one thing: proving your workout happened. Platform rules (and ours) forbid using it for ads — it's never sold, never shared. Disconnect anytime; gym-only mode works without it.

Do I need an Apple Watch or fitness band?

No — gym mode works with your phone's location alone. A watch unlocks the good stuff: outdoor sports, proof mode, and bulletproof streaks.
